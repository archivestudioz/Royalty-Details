Detailing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you come to me?

Yes, we are fully mobile and can service your vehicle at your home, workplace, or preferred location.

What happens if it rains on the day of my appointment?

We will work with you to reschedule if weather conditions prevent us from safely or properly completing the service.

Do you offer maintenance details?

Yes, we offer maintenance services for returning customers to help keep vehicles clean and protected year-round.

Do you charge extra for heavily dirty vehicles?

Vehicles with excessive pet hair, stains, sand, mold, or heavy buildup may require additional time and labor, which can affect pricing.

Will detailing remove scratches?

A standard detail will not remove deeper scratches. However, our paint correction services can significantly reduce or remove many swirl marks and light scratches.

Do you need access to water or electricity?

In most cases, yes. If access is limited, let us know ahead of time and we'll do our best to accommodate.
